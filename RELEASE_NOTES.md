Sona 1.0.92

- Chart now matches XM360/MT5 behavior:
- Timeframes M1, M5, M15, M30, H1, H4, D1, W1 (was 1m/15m/1H/4H/1D).
- Chart types: Candles, Line, Bars, and new Heikin Ashi.
- Crosshair button in the toolbar (MT5 style): tap to enable, drag to any point - price tag on the right scale, date/time tag on the bottom axis.
- Ask line with its own price tag next to the live bid line (when the feed has both sides).
- "Go to" chip appears when you scroll back - tap to jump to the newest bar.
- Pinch zoom, drag scroll, indicators, drawing tools unchanged.
- Note: Android asks once for "install apps from this source" - the dialog explains it and opens the right setting. Android's own install confirm screen then finishes the update; that tap is the system's.
